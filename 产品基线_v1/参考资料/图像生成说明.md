# 示例插画生成记录

方式：内置 image_gen 工具，未使用 CLI/API fallback。

最终文件：`../UI/assets/sample-book.png`。该文件已复制进本交付包，不依赖工具生成目录或旧项目文件。

用途：绘本示例的封面、内容图、登录页视觉。由同一新插画保持设计稿视觉一致；真实产品应替换为机构提供的对应页素材。

最终提示词：

> Use case: illustration-story. Create a polished landscape children's English picture-book illustration for the LumaRead reading-practice UI, for primary-school students aged about 6–12. A warm brown-and-white friendly dog runs toward a bright coral red ball on a spacious green lawn. Gentle rolling park hills, small flowers, leafy trees, pale blue sky. Sophisticated contemporary picture-book gouache illustration, soft paper grain, clean shapes, restrained detail, charming but not toddler-like, editorial quality. Landscape composition about 3:2. Main dog and ball fully visible and comfortably within central 70 percent so the illustration works inside card crops. Palette warm ivory, sage green, teal, coral, sunshine yellow. No text, no letters, no watermark, no UI, no device frame. This is original sample book artwork for a product design proposal, not an existing book cover.

界面精确中文、交互按钮和页面结构通过静态排版制作并导出为 PNG，不依靠生成式图像中的文字作为规范。页面源文件可供后续设计修改，但不是业务应用实现。
